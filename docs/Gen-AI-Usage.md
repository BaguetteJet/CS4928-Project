# Gen AI Usage

ChatGPT was used to analyse the original paper's implementation. It flagged that the baseline's early per-pattern cells score against k-NN neighbour-vote labels rather than ground truth, which changed how we interpret the reproduced numbers. Deepseek was used to draft the notebook cell that builds the frozen RQ1 sample folders. 

All responses were validated and decision were ultimately made by the team. All reported metrics were produced by running the notebooks ourselves.
